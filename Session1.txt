Task-4. 
Suppose you are building a new playlist recommendation feature for a Spotify-like app. List 3 
basic statistical terms (such as mean, median, mode, variance, etc.) that would be useful in 
analyzing user listening data, and explain each with a one-line example. 

Solution: 
Useful Statistical Terms for Playlist Recommendations 
1. Mean (Average): Finds the average listening time—for example, users may listen to 
music for an average of 45 minutes per day. 
2. Mode: Finds the most frequently listened-to genre—for example, if  rock is played most 
often, the app can recommend more rock songs. 
3. Variance: Measures how much listening behavior differs between users—for example, 
some users may listen for 20 minutes while others listen for 3 hours, showing high 
variance.
